# Reading Strategy

Read in this order:
1. Segmentation foundations and benchmarks: FCN, DeepLabv3+, SegFormer, Cityscapes, ACDC.
2. Shift and adaptation: DANN, ADVENT, TENT, CoTTA, EATA, SAR, RoTTA.
3. Predictive representations/world models: PlaNet, Dreamer, I-JEPA.
4. Reliability: calibration, uncertainty under shift, corruption benchmarks.
5. Recent surveys and 2024–2026 segmentation/TTA papers.

For each paper record: task, setting (offline/online; source available/source-free), labels available at test time, architecture, adaptation parameters, datasets, metrics, compute, failure modes, and relation to this proposal.

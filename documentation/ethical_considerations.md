# Ethical and Responsible Research

- Respect dataset licenses, privacy conditions and non-commercial restrictions.
- Do not publish identifiable faces, license plates or location-sensitive raw examples unless permitted and necessary.
- Do not use the system as a safety-critical autonomous-driving component.
- Report subgroup/condition failures and uncertainty.
- Document limitations, dataset biases and environmental/compute costs.
- Do not claim robustness from benchmark scores alone.

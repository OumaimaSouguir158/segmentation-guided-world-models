# BDD100K Dataset Card

Official project: https://www.bdd100k.com/  
Original paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.04687

- Purpose: large-scale diverse driving video and image benchmark.
- Use in this project: optional cross-domain/time-of-day/weather evaluation and segmentation task where labels are available.
- Access: review official terms and download instructions.
- Caution: BDD100K contains multiple tasks and annotation subsets; do not assume every image has pixel-level segmentation labels.

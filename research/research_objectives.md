# Research Objectives

1. Build a reproducible segmentation baseline.
2. Define shift labels and leakage-safe stream splits.
3. Implement three drift signals: feature, semantic and temporal predictive.
4. Evaluate score fusion and threshold calibration.
5. Implement conservative TTA with update gating and rollback.
6. Measure task performance, detection quality, calibration and efficiency.
7. Release a transparent report including negative and inconclusive findings.

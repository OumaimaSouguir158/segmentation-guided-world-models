# Scope

**Included:** semantic segmentation, urban-scene image/video streams, covariate and appearance shift, temporal latent prediction, unsupervised TTA, drift detection, computational efficiency.

**Excluded from the initial six-month scope:** action-conditioned robotics, large-scale video generation, autonomous vehicle deployment, online learning from human feedback, safety certification, and training a foundation model from scratch.

The term *visual world model* is operationalized here as a learned latent transition/prediction module over temporally adjacent visual observations. If the project later adopts a broader world-model definition, the proposal and evaluation must be revised accordingly.

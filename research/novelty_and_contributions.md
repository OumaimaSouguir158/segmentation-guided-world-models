# Novelty Assessment (Provisional)

## What is established
Semantic segmentation, temporal prediction/world models, distribution-shift detection, online/test-time adaptation, confidence filtering and continual adaptation each have substantial prior literature.

## Proposed research angle
A specific, falsifiable integration: **semantic structure + latent predictive surprise as complementary drift evidence, used to govern low-cost adaptation**.

## Claims not to make yet
- Do not claim the first segmentation-guided world model.
- Do not claim state-of-the-art performance.
- Do not claim robust real-world safety.
- Do not describe a planned module as experimentally validated.

## Required novelty check
Before submission/publication, search recent papers using combinations of: semantic-aware test-time adaptation; segmentation uncertainty drift detection; predictive residual domain shift; world model test-time adaptation; continual semantic segmentation adaptation.
